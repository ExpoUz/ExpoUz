# ExpoUz — Pitch Owner Guide (English)

A short guide for venue owners. No technical knowledge needed. Works on your phone.

---

## 1. What ExpoUz does for you
ExpoUz brings players to your court. Players find your venue in the app, book a time, and pay online.
**ExpoUz helps fill your empty slots** — you get bookings and see your earnings in one place.

## 2. How to open the Owner Portal
Open this link in your phone's browser (Chrome or Safari):

**https://pitch-portal-peach.vercel.app**

Tip: add it to your home screen so it opens like an app.
(The `@ExpoScoreBot` Telegram bot is the **player** app — the Owner Portal is the web link above.)

## 3. How to log in (first time)
1. Open the link.
2. Type your **phone number**.
3. Tap **Send code**. You get an **SMS** with a 6-digit code.
4. Type the code and tap **Sign in**.

Only owner accounts can enter. If it says access denied, contact ExpoUz (step 12) so we can set up
your owner account.

## 4. How to add your court/pitch
Adding a new venue is done **with the ExpoUz team** for now. Send us (step 12):
- Court name and address
- Sport (padel or football) and court type/surface
- Price per hour (in UZS)
- A few photos
- Amenities (parking, changing room, lights, etc.)

**What happens next:** we add your venue. It first shows as **Pending** while our team verifies it.
Once approved, it becomes **Verified** and players can see and book it. You'll see its status on the
**My Pitches** page.

## 5. How to see today's bookings
1. Open **Dashboard** — it shows this month's matches, revenue, and players at a glance.
2. Open **Schedule** — it shows all the games booked at your venues, by date, with the player list.

## 6. How to check players in when they arrive
Check-in happens **in the ExpoUz app**, done by the game's organizer when players show up. In the
Owner Portal you can always **see who is booked** for each game (open the game in **Schedule**), so
you know who to expect.

## 7. How to create your own game to fill a quiet slot
Right now, games are created in the **ExpoUz app** (the one players use). Open the app, choose your
venue, pick the time, and publish the game — players can then join. Your new game will also appear in
the Owner Portal **Schedule**.
(If you'd like help creating games, contact us — step 12.)

## 8. How to see your earnings and when you get paid
1. Open **Revenue**.
2. You'll see:
   - **Gross revenue** — total paid for games at your venues.
   - **Commission** — the ExpoUz platform fee deducted.
   - **Net revenue** — what you keep.
Money for a game is settled after the game is completed. ExpoUz then pays out your net earnings.
If you're unsure about a payout, contact us (step 12).

## 9. How commission works (simple example)
ExpoUz keeps a small **10% commission** on the court price.

Example: a court booked for **200 000 UZS**.
- ExpoUz commission (10%) = **20 000 UZS**
- **You receive = 180 000 UZS**

You always see gross, commission, and your net on the **Revenue** page — no hidden fees.

## 10. What to do if a player doesn't show up
- The booking and payment are still recorded — open the game in **Schedule** to see who booked.
- ExpoUz has a cancellation policy: if a player cancels **late (within 5 hours)** they are charged a
  **50% penalty**; a **no-show** is charged in full. This protects your slot.
- If a no-show causes a problem for you, contact us (step 12) and we'll help sort it out.

## 11. How to change your language
The Owner Portal is currently in **English**. Uzbek and Russian for the portal are coming soon.
The **player app** already supports Uzbek, Russian, and English. If you need help in Uzbek or
Russian in the meantime, contact us (step 12) — we speak your language.
